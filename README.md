# 📊 Shipment & Delivery Performance Dashboard

An interactive analytics dashboard designed to analyze **sales, order volume, shipping performance, and delivery efficiency**.

The project transforms raw shipment data into actionable business insights, helping stakeholders monitor operational performance, identify delivery issues, and understand the factors affecting sales and shipping outcomes.

---

## 🎯 Project Objective

The main objective of this project is to answer key business questions such as:

* How are total sales and orders performing?
* What is the average delivery time?
* How many orders are delayed?
* Which shipping modes generate the highest sales?
* Which payment types contribute most to revenue?
* Which products/categories have the strongest sales performance?
* Where are delivery performance issues occurring?
* How can shipment data support better operational decisions?

---

## 📌 Dashboard Overview

The dashboard is organized into multiple analytical sections:

### 🏠 Overview

Provides a high-level summary of the business through key KPIs and visualizations.

### 📦 Products

Analyzes product and category performance, including:

* Product Price
* Total Orders
* Total Sales
* Average Delivery Days
* Product-level performance

### 🚚 Shipping

Focuses on logistics and delivery performance:

* Shipping Mode
* Delivery Time
* Delayed vs. On-Time Orders
* Shipping performance comparison

### 📈 Performance

Provides a deeper view of sales and operational performance through:

* Sales trends
* Order performance
* Delivery KPIs
* Payment Type analysis
* Shipping performance

### 📋 Details

Provides a detailed view of the underlying shipment and order-level data for deeper investigation.

### Additional KPI

**Delayed Order Rate**

> Delayed Orders ÷ Total Orders

This KPI helps monitor the percentage of shipments that require attention due to delivery delays.

## 🔍 Key Insights

### 1. Delivery Performance

The dashboard highlights the relationship between **delivery time and delayed orders**, helping identify potential operational bottlenecks.

With **69 delayed orders out of 117 total orders**, delivery reliability is an important area to monitor.

### 2. Shipping Mode Performance

Sales are compared across different shipping modes to understand which delivery methods contribute most to revenue.

This analysis can support decisions around:

* Shipping method optimization
* Service-level monitoring
* Delivery cost management

### 3. Payment Type Analysis

Sales are analyzed across different payment types, allowing the business to understand customer payment behavior and revenue contribution.

The dashboard compares:

* Debit
* Transfer
* Payment
* Cash

### 4. Product Performance

Product-level analysis provides visibility into:

* High-performing products
* Sales contribution
* Order volume
* Delivery performance

This can help identify products that require additional inventory, sales focus, or operational attention.
